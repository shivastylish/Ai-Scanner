# AI Screener

> **Build with Evidence. Validate with Data. Earn Trust.**

## Overview

AI Screener is a modular, AI-assisted stock research platform for the Indian equity market.

The platform helps traders and investors identify high-quality opportunities through explainable analysis, quantitative research, historical validation, and disciplined risk management.

AI Screener is **not** an automated trading system and does **not** guarantee profits. It is designed to improve decision-making by presenting transparent evidence and highlighting potential risks.

---

# Vision

To build the most trusted AI-powered stock research platform for Indian markets.
